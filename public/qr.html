ENDO Clinic Operations

Scan to put the app on your phone and get your assignment alerts
Open the phone camera and point it at the code.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Scan the code and open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open ENDO Ops from your home screen and sign in with your staff ID.
  4. Tap Turn on notifications and choose Allow.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Scan the code and open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it appears, or ⋮ menu, then Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. Open ENDO Ops and sign in with your staff ID.
  4. Tap Turn on notifications and choose Allow.
للآيفون: فتح الرابط في Safari ← زر المشاركة ← «إضافة إلى الشاشة الرئيسية» ← فتح التطبيق وتسجيل الدخول ← «تفعيل الإشعارات».
للأندرويد: فتح الرابط في Chrome ← «تثبيت التطبيق» ← فتح التطبيق وتسجيل الدخول ← «تفعيل الإشعارات».
Notifications arrive between 7:30 AM and 4:30 PM only. iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or newer.
